# Viral Easter Browser Game Ideas 🐣🎮

This document contains Easter-themed browser game concepts designed with **viral mechanics** in mind. The focus is on:

- Simple controls
- Fast gameplay loops
- High replayability
- Funny or chaotic moments
- Shareable scores

---

# 1. Too Many Eggs

## Concept
A bunny must carry an increasingly tall stack of eggs without letting them fall.

## Gameplay Loop
1. Eggs fall from the sky.
2. They stack on top of the bunny.
3. Player moves left and right to keep the stack balanced.
4. The stack grows taller and harder to balance.

## Viral Potential
- Physics chaos creates funny moments.
- Players compete for the **highest egg stack**.
- Easy to share results like:

> "I stacked 42 eggs!"

## Extra Mechanics
- Golden eggs worth +5
- Wind that pushes the stack
- Special rare rainbow eggs

# 2. Egg Russian Roulette

## Concept
Players smash eggs on their head — some are **hard-boiled**, others are **raw**.

## Gameplay
1. Choose an egg from a group.
2. Smash it on your head.
3. If it's hard-boiled → you survive the round.
4. If it's raw → huge yolk explosion.

## Viral Potential
- Random outcomes make it exciting.
- Funny character reactions.
- Streamers and friends enjoy games with unpredictable results.

## Possible Additions
- Multiplayer mode
- Different egg types
- Dramatic slow-motion yolk splats

# 3. Don’t Wake the Chick

## Concept
A chick is sleeping inside a giant egg nest.  
You must steal eggs without waking it.

## Gameplay
1. Move quietly around the nest.
2. Grab eggs and bring them to safety.
3. Noise meter increases as you move.
4. If the chick wakes up, it chases you.

## Viral Potential
- Builds tension.
- Cute characters make it shareable.
- Funny chase scenes when the chick wakes.

## Twist
If fully awakened, the chick becomes a **rage monster** that sprints at the player.

# 4. Egg Cannon

## Concept
Launch eggs from a cannon into baskets or targets.

## Gameplay
1. Aim the cannon.
2. Adjust launch power.
3. Fire eggs toward baskets or targets.
4. Use physics to bounce eggs off objects.

## Viral Potential
- Satisfying physics-based gameplay.
- Players attempt trick shots.
- Easy level-sharing potential.

## Possible Egg Types
- Exploding eggs
- Sticky chocolate eggs
- Portal eggs
- Bouncy rubber eggs

---

# 5. Bunny Delivery Disaster

## Concept
The Easter Bunny must deliver eggs without losing them while driving across chaotic terrain.

## Gameplay
1. Drive a cart filled with eggs.
2. Navigate hills, ramps, and obstacles.
3. Eggs bounce around in the cart.
4. Lose too many eggs and the run ends.

## Viral Potential
- Physics disasters create funny moments.
- Players share their worst crashes.

## Expansion Ideas
- Multiple vehicles
- Weather hazards
- Giant stunt ramps
- Online leaderboards

# Viral Design Tips

To maximize shareability:

## 1. Fast Start
Players should understand the game in **under 5 seconds**.

## 2. Short Rounds
Ideal round length:
- 30 seconds
- 60 seconds
- 2 minutes max

## 3. Score Sharing
Example share messages:

- "You balanced **27 eggs** 🥚"
- "You woke the chick in **8 seconds** 🐣"
- "You delivered **83% of the eggs** 🧺"

## 4. Increasing Chaos
Good viral games become progressively more chaotic.

Examples:
- Faster egg drops
- More obstacles
- Unstable physics
