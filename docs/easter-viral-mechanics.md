# Viral Browser Game Mechanics for Easter Games 🐣🎮

This document lists **15 gameplay mechanics that historically perform well on browser game portals** such as CrazyGames, Poki, and similar sites.

These mechanics tend to spread because they are:

* Easy to learn
* Fast to replay
* Highly competitive
* Shareable

You can combine these with **Easter themes (eggs, bunnies, chicks, baskets, spring environments)** to create engaging seasonal games.

---

# 1. Endless Runner

## Description

The player runs automatically while avoiding obstacles and collecting items.

## Why It Works

* Instant understanding
* Endless replayability
* Natural leaderboard competition

## Easter Adaptation

* Bunny running through fields
* Collect eggs and carrots
* Avoid foxes or fences

---

# 2. Physics Stacking

## Description

Players stack objects while maintaining balance.

## Why It Works

* Physics creates funny moments
* Players compete for height records

## Easter Adaptation

* Stack eggs into tall towers
* Unstable eggs wobble and roll

---

# 3. Click / Tap Reaction

## Description

Players must react quickly when something appears.

## Why It Works

* Very simple interaction
* Highly addictive

## Easter Adaptation

* Click eggs before they hatch
* Smash eggs before they disappear

---

# 4. Catch Falling Objects

* Clear scoring system

## Easter Adaptation

* Catch falling eggs in baskets
* Avoid rotten eggs

# Tips for Designing a Viral Browser Game

## 1. Teach the Game in 5 Seconds

Players should understand the objective almost instantly.

## 2. Keep Controls Simple

Examples:

* One button
* Arrow keys
* Mouse movement

## 3. Keep Rounds Short

Ideal duration:

* 30 seconds
* 1 minute
* 2 minutes maximum

## 4. Show the Score Clearly

Players should always know how well they are doing.

## 5. Encourage "One More Try"

Difficulty should increase gradually so players feel they can beat their last score.

# Example Combined Idea

You can combine several mechanics:

Example:
"Bunny Egg Dash"

Mechanics used:

* Endless runner
* Item collection
* Dodge obstacles
* Leaderboard scoring

This combination is common in successful browser games.

---

# Final Thought

Most successful browser games are built around **one strong mechanic** rather than many complex systems.

Focus on making **one interaction extremely satisfying**, then polish it with visuals, sounds, and progression.
